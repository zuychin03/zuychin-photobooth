## Platform

web

## Users and purpose

Zuychin Photobooth serves solo photo creators, distant couples and small friendship groups, and hosts/guests at small events. People capture or import photos, finish a keepsake, and retain or share it intentionally. All three tracks are included.

## Capabilities and constraints

Preserve the existing Next.js/React/Canvas application and routes. Local projects work without an account. Cloud operations, group rooms and event publishing have distinct permission boundaries. Four live participants is the ceiling. Original photos remain separate from edit settings. Camera denial leads to import; microphone use is optional and explicit. Saved, queued, uploaded and published are different states.

## Brand commitments

Retain the Zuychin name and approved logo. Keep the rose accent, blush neutrals, partner purple and Lucide icons. Since November 2026 the UI uses Mochiy Pop One for headings and Zen Maru Gothic for controls and text (picked from rendered specimens for a soft, playful voice), and Apple Liquid Glass on the control layer only (nav, toolbars, sheets, menus, floating buttons) with content on solid grouped panels. Strip and recap canvases keep Fraunces, Geist and Geist Mono as output fonts. New project and capture surfaces are working tools inside this app, not a replacement landing-page identity.

## Evidence and decisions

The 24 generated scene and material sources are in `docs/assets/v2/`. Generated concepts do not prove customer demand. Device, physical print, hosted-provider and release checks are tracked separately.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Provide labelled touch/keyboard controls, alternatives to dragging, reduced motion/no-flash controls, legible countdowns, honest loading/error/retry states and English interface and authored prompts, with local date formatting. Preserve user-written captions without forced translation.
